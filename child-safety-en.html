VRide is intended exclusively for users aged 18 and over (see our Terms of Use). We take child safety seriously, and this page explains our approach.

Zero tolerance

VRide strictly prohibits any content, behaviour, or conduct that sexualizes, abuses, endangers, or otherwise exploits children (CSAE – Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation), including sharing, creating, or soliciting such material. Violations result in immediate and permanent account termination.

How to report a concern

Any user can report a profile, photo, or message directly in the app via the "Report" button, or block that user outright. Concerns can also be sent by email to vride@thevride.com.

What we do when a report is confirmed

We investigate every report we receive. Where a violation is confirmed, we immediately and permanently terminate the account and remove the offending content.

Reporting to authorities

Where we become aware of material that exploits children, we report it to the relevant authorities:

  • Police of the Czech Republic – aplikace.policie.cz/hotline
  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) – report.cybertip.org

If you believe a child is in immediate danger or has been the victim of abuse, please contact local law enforcement directly.

Contact

Questions about these standards can be sent to vride@thevride.com.
